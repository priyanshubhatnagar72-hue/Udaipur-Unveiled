The Royal Itinerary

Day 1: History & Heritage

Begin your day entering the City Palace through the Tripoliya Gate. Visit the nearby Jagdish Temple. Post lunch, explore the Vintage Car Museum. End your day with the cultural folk dance at Bagore Ki Haveli at Gangaur Ghat.

Day 2: Nature & Tranquility

Morning walk at Saheliyon Ki Bari. Take a boat ride to Jag Mandir island. In the evening, drive up to the Monsoon Palace for the sunset, followed by a romantic dinner at Ambrai Ghat.
